About me.

My name is Dylan Gearhart, and I love developing ideas into software.

I am a Software Developer from Kalamazoo, MI currently living in Tallahassee, FL.

My interest in Computer Science started back in 2019 when my close friend showed me a game he was developing in C++. The following year, I returned to college to study computer science and landed my first job as a part-time IT Assistant at Western Michigan University Homer Stryker M.D School of Medicine. During my time here in this role, I was exposed to a wide variety of technologies and experiences. Eventually, I was offered a role a Junior Web Developer to work on the school's E-learning platform. I view the 18 months I was employed there as an invaluable experience and look back on it very fondly. Early on during my time there, the university was targeted in a cyberattack. Seeing the fallout of such an attack firsthand made me realize the importance of Cybersecurity and how vital it is to develop secure systems in todays digital environment. Shortly after this incident, I switched my major to Cybersecurity.

Fast-forward to today, and I in my final year of school and I am currently a Software Developer Intern at Paul Consulting Group in Tallahasee, FL.

At PCG, I've had the opportunity to work with a wide range of technologies and work on different projects including refactoring legacy code used in an application that processes $34 billion annually of medicaid funds. Additionally, I am on the ground floor of the development of a brand-new web application. I have served as a critical member in delivering a major improvement to the workflow of our client, who was previously relying on an older and less robust software solution.

I've collaborated closely with my team to develop a RESTful and Progressive Web Application (PWA) using tools like .NET MVC, Angular, and .NET's Entity Framework.
